# Workout Tracker

A Home Assistant add-on for logging workouts and sports, for everyone in the house, with stats on how you're doing.

- **Users:** everyone has their own sessions, gear and stats
- **Sessions:** date, how long, how it felt, what it cost, notes, and your smartwatch's heart rate and calories
- **Gear:** what you buy for each sport, counted in your spending
- **Stats:** time, spending, how sessions felt, heart rate and streaks, for the last month, 3 months, year or all time
- **Phone and PC:** live on every screen, installable as an app

## Sports

Each sport adds its own details on top of what every session has.

| Sport | What it adds |
|-------|--------------|
| 🎾 Padel | Match or training, the score set by set with tie-breaks, result, partner and opponents, club, indoor or outdoor, and who you win with most |

More sports are on the way.

## Installation

1. In Home Assistant: **Settings → Add-ons → Add-on store → ⋮ → Repositories**, and add
   `https://github.com/christina-thd/workout-tracker`
2. Install **Workout Tracker**, start it and turn on **Show in sidebar**

See [workout-tracker/README.md](workout-tracker/README.md) for the add-on itself and
[workout-tracker/docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](workout-tracker/docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) for working on it.
